Synthetic agency and the choreography of trust

T
Dr. Thorne
Published Oct 24 · 4 min read

Robotic systems rarely ask for trust directly. They accumulate it through legible motion, predictable feedback, and the small rituals users repeat until the interface becomes part of the room.

Trust is not a feature. It is a tempo between expectation and response.
